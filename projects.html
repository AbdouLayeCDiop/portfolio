Pomodoro Timer
Utility Focus Timer Academic

Overview

Personal project based on the Pomodoro Technique, a time management method that boosts productivity by breaking work into 25-minute, highly focused intervals (called "pomodoros") separated by 5-minute breaks. Developed by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s, it encourages sustained concentration and prevents mental fatigue by requiring a longer, 15-30 minute break after every four pomodoros.
